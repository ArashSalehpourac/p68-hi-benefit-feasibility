# P68 major revision v3

Date: 2026-10-04

The manuscript was revised after R5A, R5B, and R5C. The central estimand is now explicitly limited to payload-mediated interaction under exogenous measured-network replay. The paper now uses non-anticipative feasibility-prediction terminology, includes the matched-capacity interaction ablation and positive control, reports oracle-headroom and tighter-margin sensitivity, includes exploratory 60/75/90% budgets, and reports three-way class/network-block/service-round uncertainty for the mechanism results.

Glasgow is described as an independent network-family transport of the same frozen inference workload, not a fully independent workload replication. The limitations explicitly state that the replay cannot identify endogenous coupling through location, mobility, congestion, user behavior, arrival processes, or serving load.
